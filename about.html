About BloodLink

Connecting Blood. Connecting Lives.

BloodLink is a web-based platform designed to make finding blood availability faster and easier.

BloodLink connects users with hospitals that have the required blood group available. Users can search for a blood group, view available hospitals and request blood from the selected hospital.

The platform also includes delivery-partner support to help connect blood requests with available delivery partners.
